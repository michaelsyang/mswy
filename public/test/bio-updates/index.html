Loss of BECN1 does not compromise autophagosome formation but alters cargo selection and HLA-restricted antigen presentation of B cells.
Shows BECN1/beclin-1 is dispensable for autophagosome formation itself but governs cargo selection and immune surveillance. In BECN1-knockout cells, autophagosomes form preferentially at the ER, incorporate less endosomal TLR3, yet enrich ER peptide-loading complexes, diminishing T-cell detection. Positions BECN1 as a selectivity determinant, not a formation factor.

Science advancesIF 11.7PMID 42616902
Central T cell tolerance from sparse peptide sampling.
Shows thymic negative selection achieves broad self-tolerance through machine-learning-style generalization: sparse random sampling of just 10% of self-peptides suffices to avoid reactivity to 90% of peripheral self-peptides. Validates model predictions with diverse experimental data, proposing a quantitative answer to how tolerance is generalized from the few peptides a T cell encounters in the thymus.

Science advancesIF 11.7PMID 42616901
Targeting PKGIα Cys(42) attenuates cardiac dysfunction in heart failure with preserved ejection fraction.
Identifies a druggable mechanism in heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF): the natural compound urolithin A directly oxidizes Cys42 of cGMP-dependent protein kinase Iα (PKGIα), counterintuitively activating the kinase. In a multihit HFpEF model, urolithin A improved diastolic function and attenuated remodeling via Cys42-dependent PKGIα activation, validated in human engineered heart tissue.

Unconventional role of hydroxymethylglutaryl-CoA synthase 1 in driving pathogenic T(H)17 cell immunity and autoimmune diseases.
Reveals a noncanonical role for cholesterol enzyme HMGCS1 in pathogenic T(H)17 differentiation, independent of metabolism but dependent on catalytic Cys129. HMGCS1 stabilizes IRE1α by blocking MARCH5-mediated degradation, fueling an XBP1s ER-stress response that licenses RORγt and pathogenicity. Blocking this axis mitigates autoimmune disease in vivo.

Endometriosis-derived iPSCs reveal conserved stromal maturation and endocrine responsiveness.
Establishes patient-derived iPSC lines modeling endometrial stromal differentiation in endometriosis. Differentiated cells repress pluripotency programs, gain hormone-responsive stromal signatures, and progressively resemble eutopic endometriosis stroma, while conditioned medium reprograms macrophage-like cells, providing a tractable human model for stromal-immune crosstalk.

Science advancesIF 11.7PMID 42616886
Isocaloric switch from low-fat to Western high-fat diet triggers transcriptomic reprogramming and ectopic olfactory receptor responses.
In a controlled twin study, six weeks of an isocaloric low-fat to Western high-fat diet switch (with stable body weight) raised LDL and HDL cholesterol and induced transient insulin resistance and inflammatory markers that subsided. Adipose transcriptomics showed extensive restructuring, with olfactory receptor mRNAs elevated and correlated to insulin resistance, visceral fat, and IL-18, inversely to adiponectin.

Screening of anti-metastasis drugs by targeting angiopellosis and cancer cluster extravasation.
